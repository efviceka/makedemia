Frequently asked questions

What is Makedemia?

Makedemia is project-based mentoring for students aged 12–18. Each student is matched with a mentor and builds a real project in science, engineering, AI and software, or venture-building, in addition to school.

Is there a trial before we sign up?

Yes. Start with a free 30-minute call to talk about the student's interests and which program fits. The next step is a Blueprint Session, a 60-minute session with a mentor and a written project plan within 24 hours. It is fully credited toward any program you join.

What if the student doesn't know what to build yet?

Yes. The Blueprint Session is designed for this: the mentor helps find an idea that suits your child's interests and level.

Does it require prior coding or lab experience?

No. Projects are scoped to the student's level and grow from there.

What is the difference between Build Sprint, Core Lite and Core Pro?

Build Sprint is 8 one-on-one sessions finished within 4 months, best for one focused project. Core Lite and Core Pro run for 6 months with written feedback, a monthly parent report and competition planning. Core Lite has one session a month; Core Pro has one every two weeks and suits research and ambitious builds. We recommend one on your free call.

Who are the mentors, and how are students matched?

Our mentors are PhD candidates, graduates, and professionals who studied or worked at universities and companies such as MIT, Oxford, and Intel (see the mentor profiles above). After your free call and the Blueprint Session, we match the student with the mentor who best fits their interests, goals and project, and confirm the match with you.

Does Makedemia guarantee admission to an overseas university?

No. We never promise admission, medals or publications. We promise quality mentoring and a finished, real project if the student does the work. Our focus is the project and portfolio, not the application itself, and mentors never write or build anything for the student. The school or a parent remains the official advisor for competitions and applications.

How do you keep students safe online?

Mentors are identity-checked and sign a safeguarding code. Sessions run on Makedemia links, there is no private messaging between mentors and students, and Makedemia is in every written chat.

Are the sessions online or in person?

All mentoring is online, one-on-one, in English or Bahasa Indonesia, and scheduled for Indonesia time.

Can students join as a team?

Yes. Teams of 2–4 students preparing for a team competition, such as iGEM, Technovation or the World Robot Olympiad, can share one mentor. We discuss team options and pricing on the free call.

How much time does a student need each week?

About 3–8 hours: the session plus independent work. Schedules flex around exams and schoolwork.

Can parents follow the student's progress?

Yes. Parents receive short progress updates, and Core programs include a monthly report. Makedemia is in every written chat, and there is no private messaging between mentors and students.

How much does it cost?

We share program prices on the free 30-minute call, after we understand the student's goals, so you only see the option that fits. Families who commit to 12 months save 10%.

How soon will you reply after I book?

We reply within 1 working day after you submit the booking form.
